\section{Conclusion}
PR-LFL makes the client's class prior a regularization center that the data can override, and binds the compressed updates that carry the shared model to records that can be checked after the fact. Twenty parameters and 80 B of local state lower client-macro NLL from 0.680 to 0.645 on CIFAR-10 and from 0.206 to 0.188 on FashionMNIST, and the mean gain rises as heterogeneity strengthens across a three-point sweep. Three-bit uploads cost 54,067 B, 90.1\% below dense; fifteen registrations across five clients and three rounds cost 4.23 M gas, and every resulting model matches the direct path bit for bit.
