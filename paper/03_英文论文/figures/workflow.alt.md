Panel A shows real low-bit TRAIN updates carried over loopback HTTP to a deployed single-node local Ganache EVM registry and a durable learning server. The shared SmallCNN produces each update; uniform3 with error feedback produces a 54,067-byte payload. Pending bytes and the candidate residual are persisted before transmission. HTTP authentication precedes the contract binding gate and strict decoding. The contract registers the full context and digest; canonical receipt/readback and two descendant blocks gate acceptance. SQLite atomically incorporates the weighted aggregate. Only an authenticated, session/challenge/context-bound INCORPORATED receipt authorizes EF commitment. Two original CIFAR clients and two fresh local epochs passed 128 assertions, including lost responses before local registration and after model incorporation, exact retry, service/database reopening, replay rejection and same-round missing-client recovery.

Panel B shows the core local predictor, evaluated in the full predictive experiments rather than this two-round integration. The frozen shared logits and local TRAIN class counts feed a ridge fit centered on scale one and the centered smoothed log-count prior mu. Scale A and bias b correct the center; prediction uses A times z plus b. Twenty FP32 values occupy 80 B and require no adaptation upload. The connecting arrow states the architecture.

The integration's direct and protocol arms use float64 residual/aggregation/server state, while the CNN and codec use FP32. Their bitwise agreement does not establish equivalence to the main FP32/MPS training trajectory. The trusted server, keys, SQLite authority, EVM bridge and correct decoder remain in scope. Single-node contract execution and manually mined descendants do not establish multi-node consensus, public-chain finality, an embedded model watermark, poisoning defense or honest-training proof.
