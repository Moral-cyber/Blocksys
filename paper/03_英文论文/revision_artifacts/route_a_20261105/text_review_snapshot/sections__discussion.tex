\section{Discussion}
Three observations explain why 20 parameters close this much of the gap. Counts supply the center, fitting corrects it, and the zero-center control isolates information from capacity. The one-epoch 650-parameter Linear control uses over 32 times as many values but has higher NLL; eleven-value BCTS approaches vector scaling with lower client ECE. The useful choice is the center across compact score families.

The construction assumes the local prior is stable between fitting and serving. Primary TEST uses one partition per task; the frozen-predictor development reallocation diagnostic reverses the NLL gain as label distributions drift (Appendix~\ref{app:sensitivity}). Client-macro gains are larger than sample-weighted gains; weighted CIFAR accuracy, Fashion's lower client-accuracy decile, and one CIFAR seed's accuracy favor the zero-center control, locating the consistent benefit in NLL.

The accounting separates three costs that deployments trade differently. Personalization adds 80 B locally, one TRAIN forward pass and a twenty-dimensional convex solve. Shared training requires 54,067 B uploads, 547,496 B FP32 residuals and dense downlinks; no further model upload is needed.
