\section{Conclusion}
PR-LFL turns the client's class prior from a fixed correction into a regularization center that the data can override. The fitted bias satisfies a stationarity identity, and its objective places fixed prior addition and unpenalized vector scaling at opposite penalty limits; a class-permutation control attributes the gain to alignment rather than capacity; and the predictor survives three-bit uploads at 54,067 B with 80 B of local state. A natural next step is to re-estimate the center when a client's label distribution drifts, leaving the shared model untouched.
