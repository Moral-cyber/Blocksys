Two panels compare prior-centered prediction gains. In the left three-point sweep, CIFAR-10 and FashionMNIST mean gain is greatest at alpha0.1 and falls at alpha0.5 and1.0; errors show seed sample SD. The right panel plots all twenty client means per task against training sample count on a base-10 logarithmic axis, with hollow seed observations; Spearman rho is -0.771 for CIFAR-10 and -0.729 for FashionMNIST. Positive delta means lower NLL under prior centering.
