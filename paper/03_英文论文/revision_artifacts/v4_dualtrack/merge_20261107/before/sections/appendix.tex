\subsection*{Implementation}\label{app:implementation}
SmallCNN has two $3\times3$ convolutions (16/32 channels), ReLU/pooling and 64 hidden units. FP64 Newton fitting uses analytic derivatives, at most 50 steps, Armijo $10^{-4}$, 40 halvings, projected-gradient fallback and $10^{-8}$ tolerance; centered states are stored in FP32. Training uses $\operatorname{CE}(z-s_i,y)$, $s_{ic}=\max(n_{ic},1)^{-1/4}$~\cite{zhang2022fedlc}. Error feedback sends decoded $q_i^t$ from $u_i^t=\Delta_i^t+r_i^t$ and commits $r_i^{t+1}=u_i^t-q_i^t$ after incorporation; aggregation weights are $|D_i|/\sum_j|D_j|$. Uniform3 uses 256-coordinate blocks, codes $-3,\ldots,3$, a width byte, FP32 scale and 64-byte header. Four refinements follow maximum-magnitude initialization; lengths/codes/scales/padding are validated. Its $136{,}874\times3/8+535\times(1+4)+64=54{,}066.75$ B rounds to 54,067 B.

\paragraph{Recoverable upload implementation.}
Length-prefixed HMAC-SM3 authenticates bytes/context~\cite{rfc2104,shen2018sm3}; Solidity digest registration~\cite{kim2020blockfl}, Ganache readback and SQLite gate incorporation/residual commitment. HMAC receipts permit retries. The two-client/two-round integration uses FP32 CNN/codec and double protocol state: 54,408 B/message (341 B context/tag), 217,632 B total. Server, bridge, keys, database and decoder are trusted; the MAC authenticates externally.
